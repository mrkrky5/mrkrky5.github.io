Support

Need help with For the Next One on iPhone?

Contact

Email: karakayagames.support@gmail.com

Please describe the issue and where it happened in the story. There is no account or login needed to play.

Helpful information to include

  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • App version / TestFlight build, if known
  • Scene or chapter, if known
  • What happened and what you expected to happen
  • Steps to reproduce the problem

Do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information.

Progress and saves

Progress and manual saves are stored locally on your device. The app does not currently offer cloud-save synchronization. Deleting the app may remove local progress.

Advertising and privacy

If you have questions about ads or privacy choices, include them in your message. See the Privacy Policy for details.
