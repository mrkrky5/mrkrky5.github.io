Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS game For the Next One handles information, including local game saves and advertising where available.

1. Developer and contact

The app is developed by independent developer Emre Karakaya. For questions or privacy requests, contact karakayagames.support@gmail.com.

2. Accounts and gameplay data

No account or login is needed to play. The app stores manual saves, automatic progress, language and audio preferences on your device. The developer does not operate a game server that receives your saves, and the app does not provide cloud-save synchronization.

Local progress generally remains on your device until the app is removed or its stored data is cleared. Starting a new game can replace automatic progress while leaving manual slots intact. Uninstalling the app may delete local saves.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Versions of the app that include advertising use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display interstitial ads at suitable breaks in gameplay. Ad serving may involve Google processing technical information such as IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device or advertising identifiers where permitted, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data. Exact practices depend on your device, region and privacy choices.

Google may process information under its own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent limited or non-personalized ads or all technical processing. The developer does not independently sell personal information.

4. Consent and privacy controls

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may present a privacy message with consent and preference options before eligible ad requests. Where offered in the app, you can reopen privacy choices through Settings. Declining optional advertising consent does not block access to the story.

Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is separate from UMP consent. If tracking that requires ATT is used, the relevant iOS system permission is requested. You may control tracking permission in iOS Settings.

5. Other analytics and support

The app does not require a developer-operated analytics account. Google Mobile Ads may generate ad-related diagnostics when advertising is enabled. If you voluntarily email support, your email address and message are used to answer your inquiry and retained only as reasonably necessary to handle it and maintain appropriate records.

6. Retention and deletion

You can remove locally saved game information by deleting the app or clearing its app data where supported. For deletion requests about correspondence held by the developer, contact karakayagames.support@gmail.com. Google's independent processing is governed by its own terms, controls and retention policies.

7. Children and international processing

The app is not designed as a child-directed service. Advertising providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence, subject to applicable rules and safeguards.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features, third-party SDKs, or legal obligations change. Changes will be reflected by the last-updated date above.
